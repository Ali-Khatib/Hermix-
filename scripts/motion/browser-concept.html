Why a shady street feels cooler

Trees change more than the view. They change how a city feels on a hot day.
Mara Ellis · 18 June 2026 · Fictional article

Trees cool streets in two ways. Their leaves block sunlight before it reaches the pavement. They also release water vapour, which carries heat away as it evaporates.

Shade now. Cooling that lasts.

Young trees offer little shade at first. A lasting benefit needs space for roots, water and regular care, not just more planting.

The takeaway: choose trees that suit the street, then plan for their care.

In the Hermix preview app

The Windows app stores your settings and saved items on your computer so they’re there next time. This storage never leaves your device and isn’t used for tracking. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling Hermix removes it.

NamePurposeKept
hermix.v1 (app storage)Your settings, such as theme, motion, and mascot, plus your saved requests, meeting briefs, and reminders.Until you clear it or uninstall the app

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or anything else that isn’t strictly necessary, we’ll update this page first. Where the law requires it, we’ll also ask for your consent before turning it on, and give you a way to change your mind.

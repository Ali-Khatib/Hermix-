Please don’t use Hermix, this website, or our forms to:

  • Break the law, or help anyone else break it.
  • Harass, threaten, or abuse anyone, including our team.
  • Pretend to be someone else, or sign other people up for the waitlist without their permission.
  • Get in where you shouldn’t. That includes guessing passwords, stealing credentials, probing for weaknesses outside our responsible disclosure guidelines, or trying to read data that isn’t yours.
  • Spread malware or other harmful code, or send us malicious files or links.
  • Automate abuse, such as bulk or scripted submissions to our forms, scraping, or anything that overloads or disrupts our services.
  • Send spam or unsolicited bulk messages.
  • Infringe other people’s rights, including copyright, trademarks, or privacy.
  • Get around limits we put in place, such as rate limits or access controls.

Reporting misuse

If you see something that breaks these rules, use our contact form and choose “Report misuse”. Include as much detail as you can. For security problems, please follow the security reporting steps instead.

What we may do

If someone breaks these rules, we may take proportionate steps, depending on how serious it is. These include ignoring or deleting submissions, blocking further submissions, removing an address from the waitlist, or stopping someone’s access to the preview. Where the law requires it, we may report illegal activity to the authorities.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Straight answers about what Hermix does today and what’s coming.

What is Hermix?

Hermix is an AI coworker for Windows, being built to turn scattered updates, commitments, and meetings into clear plans and useful follow-through: briefings, a realistic plan, prepared replies, and meeting context. See a day with Hermix.

Is the preview live?

No. This site shows a sample day so you can see the experience. The briefings, tasks, replies, and answers in it are examples, not pulled from real accounts or a live AI model.

Does Hermix work with my apps yet?

Not yet. The preview shows a sample day. Live Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, and GitHub connections are on the way.

Will Hermix send emails or post messages for me?

Not in the preview. Nothing in it is sent or posted. Sending and posting are being built around clear permissions, so you decide what Hermix can do.

Can Hermix join my meetings?

Not yet. The sample shows meeting prep: who’s coming, what was decided last time, and what’s still open. Hermix is also being built to turn meeting records into summaries and follow-ups. Joining calls is planned.

Where is my data stored?

In the preview, everything you save stays on your computer. Hermix doesn’t send it to us or to an AI service. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 11. Hermix uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which Windows 11 already includes.

How much does it cost?

Exploring the sample preview is free. Prices for the full release haven’t been set yet. See Pricing.

How do I get Hermix?

Join the waitlist and we’ll email you when it’s ready to download.

Can I turn off the mascot or the animations?

Yes. In Settings you can hide Hermix, turn off eye tracking, and reduce motion. Hermix also follows your system’s reduced-motion setting.

How do I leave the waitlist?

Use the contact form and choose “Remove me from the waitlist”.

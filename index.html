A day with Hermix

Six places Hermix helps, in the order your day happens.

Listening

01 Today

Start the day in one sentence.

Hermix opens with a short briefing in plain words: your meetings, your free time, and the emails waiting on you. Tap any line to jump straight there.

02 What’s new

Catch up without the endless scroll.

See what changed in Gmail, Slack, GitHub, and Calendar since you last looked. What people sent sits on one side, what Hermix drafted on the other, so it’s always clear who wrote what.

03 Tasks

Know what matters next.

Commitments buried in threads become a short list. Each task gets a brief in normal English and a button that opens the right place. Finish one and it moves to Finished, with Undo.

04 Email

Replies, drafted and waiting for you.

Hermix separates what needs a reply from what’s just for your information, and writes a first draft in context. You read it, change it, and decide when it goes.

05 Meetings

Walk into every meeting prepared.

For each meeting: who’s coming, what you decided last time, what’s still open, and what to bring. Save the brief on your device and come back to it whenever you need.

Joining live calls is on the way. For now, Hermix gets you ready.

06 Ask Hermix

Just ask.

Ask what needs a reply or what to remember today. Hermix answers first, then offers the place to go, or a reminder for tomorrow.

Meet Hermix

One coworker.
Seven moods.

Hermix dresses for the job, so you can tell what it’s doing at a glance. A detective hat while it checks, a tie for meeting prep, an envelope for your inbox, a scarf when it’s time to rest.

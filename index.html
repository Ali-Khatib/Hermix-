Listening

01 Your day

One briefing instead of forty notifications.

Hermix reads everything that arrived while you were away and writes it up: what changed, what needs an answer, what can wait. Two minutes, instead of twenty tabs.

02 What changed

Catch up on a long thread in one read.

Decisions get made, then reversed, in threads you were only copied on. Hermix follows them and tells you where things actually landed and what it changes for you.

03 Your plan

Every promise you made, in one list.

Requests, commitments and follow ups become tasks, ordered by what is late and who is waiting on you. The list keeps itself current as new things come in.

04 Replies

The reply is written before you open it.

Hermix separates what needs you from what does not, drafts an answer in your own words, and waits. You read it, change what you want, and send it.

05 Prep

Walk in knowing what was decided last time.

Before a meeting, Hermix pulls the history, the open questions and the decisions already made into one page. Afterwards it turns what was said into tasks.

06 Ask

Ask about your own work, not the internet.

“What am I waiting on?” “What did I agree to this week?” “Who never answered?” Hermix answers from everything it has already read, and gives you the next step.

Listening

01 Briefings

Pick up where things stand.

Get a briefing drawn from the sources you connect, with the decisions, requests and changes that matter to what you’re doing. Open the source when you need the detail.

02 Updates

Know what changed and what it affects.

A decision in one conversation can change the work happening elsewhere. Hermix brings the relevant updates together so you can see what needs a different approach.

03 Tasks

Give the next step some context.

Turn a request into a task with the background attached. Hermix helps work out what comes next and carries out supported steps, with approval where needed.

04 Replies

Reply with the background already in place.

Prepare an answer using the conversation and the details behind it. Review the draft, make your changes and approve it before it is sent.

05 Meetings

Walk in knowing what was decided last time.

Bring earlier decisions and open questions into the next meeting. Where meeting capture is supported and enabled, keep the notes and follow-up work connected afterwards.

06 Ask Hermix

Ask a question. Give it something to do.

Ask what changed, investigate a question or hand over a task. Hermix uses the context it can access and tells you when it needs another source or your approval.

07 AI models

Your models. Your limits.

Connect Groq or Anthropic with your own API key and manage your usage limits in Hermix. Model connections are separate from the conversation that starts your setup.

08 Browser extension

Call Hermix into the page you are working on.

A detail looks wrong. Call Hermix from the toolbar, put the issue in focus and carry the question into Ask Hermix. See a suggested next step with the page as context.

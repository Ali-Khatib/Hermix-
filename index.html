Listening

01 Your day

A briefing that tells you what matters.

Hermix pulls in everything coming at you and reads it so you do not have to. You get a clear account of your day in language you understand: what changed, what needs you, and what can wait.

02 What changed

Catch the meaning, not every notification.

Requests, decisions and updates arrive from all around. Hermix brings the important ones forward and explains what happened, so you never have to rebuild the story across your apps.

03 Your plan

A plan for everything you owe.

Requests, promises and follow ups become a task list you can trust. Hermix highlights what is urgent, puts things in order, and starts working through them with you.

04 Replies

Know what needs a reply. Start with one ready.

Hermix separates the conversations that need you from the updates that do not, and prepares a reply you can read, change and approve.

05 Prep

Know what led here. Leave knowing what’s next.

Before a conversation, Hermix gathers the context, the open questions and the decisions made before. Afterwards it helps turn what was said into clear next steps.

06 Ask

Ask with the context already there.

“What am I waiting on?” “Help me prepare for this call.” “What did I agree to do?” Hermix answers from everything it has read and turns the answer into a next step.

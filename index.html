Listening

01 Today

One connected view. A clear plan for your day.

Hermix reads across your inbox, calendar, and messages, then opens with a short briefing in plain words: your meetings, your free time, and what’s waiting on you. Tap any line to jump straight there.

02 What’s new

Your apps hold the information. Hermix connects the dots.

See what changed in Gmail, Slack, GitHub, and Calendar since you last looked, in one place instead of four tabs. What people sent sits on one side, what Hermix drafted on the other, so it’s always clear who wrote what.

03 Tasks

Know what matters next.

Promises buried in threads, meetings, and updates become one short list, with what matters most at the top. Each task gets a brief in normal English and a button that opens the right place. Finish one and it moves to Finished, with Undo.

04 Email

Your next reply, already drafted.

Hermix separates what needs a reply from what’s just for your information, and writes a first draft with the context already in it. No blank page. You read it, change it, and decide when it goes.

05 Meetings

Walk into every meeting prepared.

Before each call, Hermix pulls together who’s coming, what you decided last time, what’s still open, and the talking points worth bringing up. Save the brief on your device and come back to it whenever you need.

Joining live calls is on the way. For now, Hermix gets you ready.

06 Ask Hermix

Always know your next move.

Ask what needs a reply or what to remember today. Hermix answers first, then offers the next step: the place to go, or a reminder for tomorrow.

Carrying out approved actions in your apps is on the way.
